About AgentA

AgentA · Loona Automation

AgentA is the member of the Loona family that does not just answer a question — it takes on a whole job. You describe the work in your own words, AgentA shows you a step-by-step plan first, and only when you press Start does it carry out each step in turn and hand you back one finished result.

What AgentA actually does

Most chat tools give you a single reply and leave the organising to you. AgentA works the other way round. It breaks your request into ordered steps and works through them one at a time, showing its progress as it goes. You can watch each step complete, stop the agent mid-run, ask it to continue the remaining steps, or start over with an edited task. When the run finishes you can copy the result, download it as a Markdown file, or listen to it read aloud.

Jobs people hand to AgentA

The homepage includes ready-made agent jobs: tap one and the full instruction is filled in for you. They include a Morning Planner that lays out a full day hour by hour, a Social Media Content Pack with a week of posts, hooks and captions, a Research Report on any topic, a Lesson Plan Maker with a quiz, a Business Email Writer, a Customer Reply + Lead Score that answers a customer in their own language and rates the lead Hot, Warm or Cold, a Website Builder Brief, a Story Writer, a Video Script Writer and a Business Idea Validator. You can also describe any task of your own in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

You stay in control — AgentA never sends anything

Nothing runs until you have reviewed the plan and pressed Start. If a job involves writing an email or a message, AgentA produces the draft for you to check; it does not send email, post to your accounts, message your customers, book anything or buy anything on its own. Sending, posting and paying always remain your job, done by your own hand. AgentA also cannot log in to your accounts, and it has no access to your inbox, your files or your bank.

How it works, honestly

When you submit a task, the text is sent from this page to the Loona Engine, an automation workflow that Loona Automation runs on Make.com (EU1), which plans the job and works the steps with an AI model and returns the output to your screen. AgentA is therefore not an offline or browser-only tool: it needs an internet connection, and your task wording travels to that engine to be processed. The list of your past tasks, however, is saved only on this device, as the homepage states. Please do not paste passwords, bank details or other people's private information into a task.

Limits worth knowing

AgentA can misunderstand a vague instruction, and AI output can contain mistakes — check facts, figures and names before you use a result for anything important. It cannot browse the live web for today's prices or news, and a long job may occasionally stop part-way if the engine is busy; the Continue button usually picks up where it left off.

Who AgentA is for

Teachers preparing plans and quizzes, small business owners who need a week of content or a polished customer reply, students structuring a report, and anyone who thinks in outcomes rather than prompts.
