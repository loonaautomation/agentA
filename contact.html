The fastest way to reach the team behind AgentA is by email: loonaautomation@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within 1–2 working days. Please put AgentA in your subject line, and if your message is about a specific run, paste in the exact task wording you used and tell us which step the agent stopped at — that detail solves most problems in one reply instead of three.

What to write to us about

Write to us if a task produced a wrong or unusable result, if one of the ready-made jobs (Morning Planner, Lesson Plan Maker, Customer Reply + Lead Score and the rest) does not fit the work you actually do and you want a new one added, if you would like an agent like AgentA built around your own business process, or for any question about how AgentA handles your task text. Feature ideas from real users have shaped AgentA more than anything else, so practical suggestions are genuinely welcome.

Try these first — common AgentA fixes

The plan never appears. AgentA plans through the Loona Engine, so it needs a live connection: check your internet, wait a few seconds and press “Make a plan” again; a busy engine occasionally needs a second attempt. A step failed or the run stopped early. Use “Continue remaining steps” before starting over — completed steps are kept. The result is in the wrong language. AgentA replies in the language of your task, so write the task itself in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu as you prefer the answer. Your past tasks disappeared. The task history is saved only on this device, so clearing browser data removes it; the results you downloaded as files are unaffected.

Before you email

Many quick questions are already answered on the AgentA FAQ page — including what AgentA can and cannot do, and what happens to your text. If your answer is not there, email us and we will help.
