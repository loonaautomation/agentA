AgentA — Frequently Asked Questions

AgentA · Loona Automation

Is AgentA free to use?

Yes. AgentA is free, with no payment and no premium tier blocking the agent. The site is supported by Loona Automation and may show advertising, but running a task costs you nothing.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the page, describe your task, review the plan and press Start. Your list of past tasks is stored in this browser on this device only.

How does AgentA work?

You describe a job in plain words and press “Make a plan”. AgentA shows a step-by-step plan first; nothing runs until you press Start. It then works through the steps one by one and combines the step outputs into one finished result, which you can copy, download as a Markdown file, or listen to.

What happens to the text I type?

Your task text is sent from this page to the Loona Engine, an automation workflow Loona Automation runs on Make.com (EU1), where an AI model plans and works on it and returns the result. AgentA is not a browser-only tool, so please do not include passwords, bank details or other people's private information in a task.

Can AgentA send emails or act on my accounts?

No. AgentA never sends email, posts, messages, books or buys anything on its own, and it cannot log in to any of your accounts. If a job involves an email or message, AgentA prepares the draft and you review and send it yourself.

What kinds of tasks is AgentA good at?

Structured, multi-step writing and planning work: a morning plan, a week of social media posts, a research report, a lesson plan with a quiz, a polished business email, a customer reply with a lead score, a website brief, a short story, a video script or a business-idea check. Ready-made versions of all of these are on the homepage.

What can AgentA not do?

It cannot browse the live web for today's news or prices, work without an internet connection, guarantee that AI output is factually correct, or take real-world actions for you. Always check important facts and figures in a result before relying on them.

Which languages does AgentA understand?

You can write your task in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, and AgentA replies in the same language as your task.
